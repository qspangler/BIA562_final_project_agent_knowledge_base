# D. Synthetic Data Disclaimer
All organizations, teams, venues, artists, competitors, events, prices, sales figures, policies, people, and observations in this knowledge base are **fictional** and were created solely for testing an AI-augmented pricing decision-support system (PricePilot). Any resemblance to real entities or real market prices is coincidental. The data must not be used for real pricing decisions, and no conclusion drawn from it applies to any real market. PricePilot outputs based on this data are test artifacts; final decisions in any real deployment remain with human pricing leadership.
